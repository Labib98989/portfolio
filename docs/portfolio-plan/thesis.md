# Portfolio Thesis

## The pitch
A showcase, not a résumé. The job of the portfolio is to demonstrate that I can
ship in any domain of software engineering. Each of the 9 chapters is evidence
in service of that single claim.

**Center of gravity:** applied AI (current obsession).
**But the headline is the breadth** — game engines, embedded audio, security
tooling, dev tooling, mobile, backend, hardware. AI is what I'm doing now; it's
not the only thing I do.

## Audience
Anyone. Explicitly not FAANG-targeted. Founders, clients, collaborators, curious
engineers. Visitors who skim should see the breadth. Visitors who dig should
find the depth.

## Why the form fits the claim
The radial dial with 9 evenly-weighted notches reads as a **spectrum of
capability**, not a ranked highlight reel. Every notch is equal. The form
supports the thesis instead of fighting it.

## Tone
Confident, specific, slightly dry. No buzzword bingo. Constraint-honest where
the project asks for it (embedded audio is "shaky" because of the hardware
ceiling, not abandonment; HealthTech is "in design", not pretending to be
shipped).

## Risks to keep in mind
1. "I can build anything" can read as "senior in nothing" to skeptical readers.
   Case studies must be deep enough to back the claim — every chapter needs
   real engineering meat. The deep-dive page is load-bearing now.
2. Projects 2, 4, 5, 9 have meaningful AI. That's center of gravity (4 of 9)
   without being overwhelming. Position as "applied AI center of gravity"
   without pretending the rest doesn't exist.
3. Three chapters need honest framing so visitors don't bounce off the breadth
   claim:
   - **HealthTech** (in design, not shipped — architecture writeup is the case
     study).
   - **Groovebox** (shaky because of hardware limits, not abandonment — the
     constraint IS the chapter).
   - **Event app (#3)** — kept as a **placeholder slot**, consciously thin,
     marked for replacement when a stronger candidate exists. Keep its visual
     treatment quiet so the slot doesn't draw extra attention to its own
     weakness.

## Site structure (already wireframed)
- One screen, no scroll, fixed 1440×900 stage.
- Two hover zones: **Hero** (left, project chapter) and **Dial** (right, wheel).
- Click the dial → focus mode; click any notch → jump to that chapter.
- Each chapter has its own theme: background, colors, type treatment, tone.
- **Open Case Study** CTA → deep-dive page (route/format TBD).
- Header → **About** (separate surface, holds bio + credentials + contact).
