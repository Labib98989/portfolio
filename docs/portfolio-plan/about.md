# About Page

The About surface holds the actual *me* — bio, credentials, contact. The wheel
is the work; About is the person behind it.

## What it holds
- Real bio (content TBD).
- Tagline that frames the AI center of gravity + breadth.
- Security credentials — CTF placements + hobby framing.
- Contact / links.

## Tagline direction
Lead with the AI obsession, immediately undercut with breadth. Draft:

> Currently obsessed with applied AI. Also writes game engines, breaks
> sandboxes, and squeezes 10k users out of free tiers.

That's a draft — the real one tightens after we lock the project labels.

## Security framing — what we say
- **CTF placements:** list the named events with positions.
- **Hobby framing:**
  > Spends weekends breaking sandbox isolation for fun — VirtualBox would
  > prefer I find a different hobby.
- This tells visitors the depth exists *without* attributing specific work
  back to me.
- Adds credibility to the rug-pull detector chapter (#1) without doubling up
  the security slot in the wheel.

## What we DON'T say
- No specific vulnerabilities.
- No bug-bounty attribution.
- No claims that map back to identifiable targets.
- No "I exploited X at Y" — even if true, even if cool.

The CTF wins and the sandbox-breaking line are the most we put on stage. The
rest stays off-stage by design.
